Methodology and limitations

The equal-weight buy-and-hold v1 benchmark is fully invested and does not use the Strategy's exposure or risk profile. It compares outcomes, not equal risk. Entry fees and simulated entry slippage are included; no hypothetical exit cost is deducted. This is not alpha or a profitability guarantee.
